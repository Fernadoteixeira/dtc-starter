# Product pricing workflow

1. Confirm product attributes from physical evidence; label visual inference.
2. Compute complete cost: materials, waste, labor, finishing, packaging, fees and overhead.
3. Calculate a sustainable floor with:
   `price = complete_cost / (1 - fees - target_margin)`.
4. Research three market bands: generic/economic, comparable artisan, premium ceiling.
5. Produce validation, neutral and premium scenarios.
6. Define a demand index using qualified conversations, reservations and paid orders.
7. Reprice future lots, not already committed orders.
8. State which unconfirmed attributes must not appear in the listing.
