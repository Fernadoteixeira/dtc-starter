# Quality Model

Every atomic skill is evaluated across ten dimensions:

1. Outcome clarity
2. Evidence quality
3. Boundary precision
4. Decision quality
5. Executability
6. Verification strength
7. Safety and compliance
8. Operability
9. Reversibility
10. Ownership and governance

## Scoring

- `0`: absent
- `1`: implicit
- `2`: documented
- `3`: executable
- `4`: verified
- `5`: measured and governed

A production-grade skill execution should score at least 4 in all material dimensions.
